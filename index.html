01 Mission profile

A student who keeps an eye on the sky.

I'm Jannish, a 2nd-year B.Tech student in Computer Science and Business Systems (CSBS) from Padappai, Chennai. I'm fascinated by the point where software meets space — so I build tools that pull live orbital data, propagate satellite positions with SGP4, and turn that math into something anyone can see.

CSBS lets me combine core computer science with business and management thinking. Outside class I experiment with full-stack web apps and 3D WebGL interfaces, and I'm looking for internships, hackathons and projects where I can learn by building.
